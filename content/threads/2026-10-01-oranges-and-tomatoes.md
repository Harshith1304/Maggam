Start practicing the habit of not comparing ourselves with others life. that guy might be under different circumstances, different lifestyle so his path and ambitions are definitely different from yours. His struggle is no same as yours and spent hell amount of time, efforts to getting there and there are cases where that lifestyle are a results of his family, ancestry. So it is not advisable to compare your struggle to his/her. It is making laughingstock of your efforts by sweating over others life. fight your own war not others. win at that instead of whining about others surmounts. build yourself bulletproof of adverse circumstances that includes someone winning at their life. there is no need to chase what others chase. chase things that you are longing, willing to chase even if you get ridiculed for it, bashed for it. stick to it like your entire existence depends on it. believe it long enough until it gets reflected in every tiny action you take, every decision you make. get comfortable by being shamed by others by calling you delusional and other names. forward in the direction of your goals no matter the situation, threats and criticism if you strongly believe that you are going in the intended direction of your ambitions. Build audacity around your upskilling and your development cause it induces confidence and intent to do more what you are doing. It basically enables you to work even harder to stand up/live up to the comments that you made regarding your upscaling. Audacity comes with responsibility, accountability and walking the talk. Make sure to act in line with the expectations and remarks that you made. Find the unique YOU by paving the path that isn't bred by others influences. Find only inspirations and ideas that might resonate with you but not the whole path as it lacks authenticity. Don't mimic other's advancements just cause that he/she is getting recognition, attention which may or may not that he/she deserves because doing so it might lead losing you, lack of joy in that path, lack of contentment and feeling of achievement. as it is same as planting oranges in the paddy field which makes no sense and certainly 0 yield. All it matters is YOU being the same person before, after and throughout the journey of acing Your Goal. There is no need of chasing recognition, attention and audience if at all it is what gets you to the endpoint. You are fighting this war for your own peace but for others entertainment nor attention. Be comfortable with this fact as you drift along the desired path. Silent hurdle leads to astounding Victory. let this serve as a remainder to stick to your goals if you are feeling you are not getting applause treatment from audience. Don't take lack of recognition as sign of not doing things smoothly. It just people care about the results and outcomes rather than boring, mundane persistent efforts. Answer them with your befitting victory, let there shock, surprise and envy among them of your advancement. which is a sign of winning in your game, war and thereby life.
