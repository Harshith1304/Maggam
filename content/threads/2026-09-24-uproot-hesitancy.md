BE the one who make prompt decisions and ditches hesitations. Hesitation is worse than failure cause it indicates cowardice and thereby stagnates our growth. Fail in order to gain wisdom on cracking it next time so that you are far ahead than earlier or if halted by indecision. IT is always a winner who is rather comfortable failing many times than hesitated to make a move. Imprint a move be it slopy or imperfect. let there be movement in spite of adversary, less apparatus as you gain and become stronger along the way. Organize your plans and stick to it as you walk in that path. Let it sink in and make wonders as you avoid not being consistent.
