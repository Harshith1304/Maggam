its always and should be competition with you. You are the yours biggest rival and at the same time greatest cheerleader. Become rival or cheerleader based on the circumstances. You are to be blamed for your downfall and you alone should be praised for your ascension(and it goes without saying god too is responsible). You are the result of your decisions you took and actions you have taken so far. You face the accountability for the situations it has sprouted due to your involvement. Try being responsible gentleman by taking responsibility for outcomes that happened due to your intervention nor your whole presence. take blame or praise for things that happened in your control rather than sweating over things that aren't in your control. Focus on what can be done in your ability and give best and be prepared to take whole shame or applause for the results. Its not in your control or you cant control what might unravel of certain actions but you are in full control on how to respond to aftermath of actions, which thereby tests your character, composure and rationale. Stop running from taking care of things, face them, head to head in spite of backlash, awkwardness. its never about others approval, rather its always should concern you and your self-interest. Protect your energies and fire them where it should be projected to pave towards success in ones occupation or field of desired.
