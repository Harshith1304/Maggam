the strongest of all energies, emotions and feelings is of Sexual Drive. On one hand It is the driving force for most of all the successful people and on other hand it is the destructive force for men who became slaves of it. You either become a slave to it or you can make it your slave. The later part is what makes a person achieve what he wants in his life. Whereas the earlier part is what makes a person fall to the stoop of his life. You can't eliminate or subdue the sexual desire but by channeling it into efficient outlets, it can magnify the output of your efforts. In order to achieve the compounding effect of your sexual desire in an efficient and appropriate manner, it is to transmute it into the useful means of achieving the things you desire instead of letting it out into the usual physical means of not-worthy outlets. Never let it overtake you, as it is fatal to your career and it may drain your energies, mood, and make you even less attractive in some cases. But the channeling or transmutation of sexual drive requires a huge amount of willpower, as you may fail in your first attempt. So practice regularly of conversion this energy to maximize or level up your potential thereby it translates to habit, ritual. upon reaching this phase you don't need to force upon your willpower to enforce this cycle, it happens in natural and involuntary manner. So basically, on transmuting this sexual drive into a goal-driven force you get a chance to discover many new ideas through infinite intelligence, as Napoleon Hill said. Most of the successful men are the ones who have exercised their sexual drive in much more efficient channels of transmutation. A man with no sexual drive possesses no potential for achieving greater heights in his life. Even if so, it feels void and not joyful. And a secret ingredient here is that mixing the sex with love will enable a man to reach the heights where an ordinary man can never even imagine or reach. sex and love can be a boon or a curse based on the usage of the man one possesses. Rational usage of these two ingredients can one make a man achieve things which cant be secured even with all the forces out there combined. Never underestimate the power of sex and love. As there is a physics law that states where potential energy can be converted to kinetic energy and vice versa, likewise, sexual energy can be converted to a greater potential energy (on tapping of it can one harness success) but not vice versa. Because sexual energy cannot be transferred or gained from many external sources, it comes from within. It's a state of mind. It's a natural instinct to feel sexual urges. But it is commendable of a man who uses these urges to satisfy his desires i.e. money, fame, success to become the man he wanted to become. It is this sexual drive that has led the man of bygone ages to hunt and bring the spoils to the home in order to impress his woman. If not for this woman there is no interest for him to go on and do the great things. This interest can be seen in many successful people. They earn money, bring cars, and buy fancy things just to impress these women. This keen interest and burning desire to make these women love him have led to achieve many great things and to doing so even in the future. So we can say that driving force beyond many men, especially successful people boils down to two things: love and sex. Don't be ashamed of the sexual drives that you experience or encounter. Don't try to subdue them or resist them. Just embrace them and try to change the way it tries to get out of you. Make them get out of you by doing things that must be done instead of what they desire to be done. Maintain healthier reserves of sexual desire and make great use of them by projecting them in efficient and worthy methods/outlets.
