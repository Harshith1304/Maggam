"Fortunes are suggestions to mind in a disguise". People fears will the oracle said becomes true? what if it really happened? is there no option? thereby instilling these negative suggestions to our conscious minds slowly and repeatedly. No one is sure what future holds and what will turn it ? a palmist or oracle on seeing our face or palm deciding our fate isn't it ridiculous? as just a glance they cant forecast our future but we being unsure of it and being ignorant we just make it fit into our brains ignoring logic and reasoning. Only you know what future you can build and what you can do best of your ability and hard-work you put out there to paving the path to riches. A stranger out of blue claiming she has seen the glimpses of your future? Only fortunate care to disbelief these so called "fortunes", as they are confident of what outcomes gonna spiral out of their actions. The only time those fortunes gets realized is when we get played out by those negative hetero-suggestions a.k.a. Fortunes.

What comes ahead is decided by what we did, what we do now, what we do from now on ? Not some hideous chap drawing out sticks and papers out to make us astound and believe! Even a ounce of buying the talk they gave about our fore comings can affect us gravely as those suggestions make their way to mind thereby forming reasons and excuse to defend it, then gets osmosed into subconscious, which then takes care of transmuting that fear, thought into actual reality. Maintain a rational stature and be cautious of those who tries to hack your reasoning, thereby subconscious.
