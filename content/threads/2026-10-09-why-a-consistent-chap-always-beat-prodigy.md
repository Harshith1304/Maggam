{small} Prodigy in the sense, a guy being endowed with talents and skills since his birth. Which can be referred to as the innate talents which are bestowed upon by God. But this prodigy can be defeated by chaps who are ruthless and delusional. Because prodigy has a deep sense of superiority and an immense level of overconfidence due to his innate talent, but often he gets outsmarted by the minds of those who got skilled by daily immersive practice in that competing field. History is filled with many such examples where prodigy sometimes gets humbled when they have taken lightly of their opponent. Talented people, certain individuals, take less care of honing their skills. they do not realize this until they get beaten up when they are met with opponents who have honed and upscaled their skills by being disciplined and crazily delusional about getting what they want. History has proven many times that a delusional ambitious person with great willpower and skills always beats those who are talented. So Never take your situations and opponents were granted just because you have certain skills since Childhood, Emit immense level of pride and show off only when you are training and pushing limits on a daily basis. But it always turns bad when you just show attitude and cold-shoulders  without any proof of hard-work that you haven't done or any hardships that you haven't gone through.

Let your actions and outcomes project your worth and level rather than your talk. Simply put, "let the walk do the talk". Don't get ashamed of setbacks, Even when you know those are a consequences of your shortcomings that you didn't kill off or simply because you are overconfident that you can pull it off without treating your shortcomings.

{large} Always keep in mind that a man by who under lauds himself (publicly) always beats when met with a man who over lauds or amplifies his petty surmounts. Make sure you don't turn out into the later one.

Let your scaling's advertise you, rather than you over-boasting about yourself in public, because those who overpraise  themselves end up humbled by those who are quietly building their legacy, standards.

{small} Always moderately speak of your achievements and in line of your surmounts rather underpraises or overpraises. "Fake till you make it" occasionally may not work in our favour when dealt with situations out of reach or opponents who are crazy about winning. The stated strategy should be rephrased to "Fake only when you can make it", when its in vicinity of reach of your skills, energies. Its a realistic way of faking i.e. boasting it. Boast, praise, PR should be in the same harmony as your ability of you acing it. Thereby demonstrates a distinction and border line between a genius and a crazy ambitious lad. Become the later to attain one's desired riches.
