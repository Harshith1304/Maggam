Optimistic mindset is great. it allows us to induce ourselves with positive outlook thereby mind takes care of it output equivalent in reality. it is behind placebo effect too(kind of) and makes us be joyful in dire situation. but anything in limit is elixir beyond limit it is always fatal/poisonous. so have optimistic framework that is directly proportional to the effort, lifestyle you are pouring in your pursuit of your ambitions. so less the effort less the optimism. because inverse of it will lead to dissatisfaction and ambiguity issues. so make sure to put more efforts in order to be brutal optimistic. It is okay to delusional optimism but dare to pour in great amounts of efforts, hard work and smart work. so to be clear have a realistic optimism mindset. dream of cars, watches, rich lifestyle when yours's actions are resonating in the same frequency as your dreams. Decide for yourself will that dream be a dream, a distant infinity thing?? let your actions propel towards it each day without any hiccups. Let your actions decide your level of optimistic you're gonna maintain.
