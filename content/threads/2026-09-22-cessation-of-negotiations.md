##

Stop worrying about the things that you did wrong in the past. just accept them as it was you who did it, take accountability for it cause it is you who did that, it happened on your consent, decisions and ability. Nor worry about things that has happened yet and have no clue on how its gonna spiral out in future. Coming to the present, stop negotiations with your brain on delaying that move you are supposed and thought of doing it long ago. negotiations leads to procrastinations, dilation of reaching of goals. so next time when you are not feeling it, or ill or of any bad pissed off moment just avoid playing the negotiator role. Drop the act and steer the handle, wheels will follow in sync. Stop choking on taking decision. take appropriate decision even when you are not inspired, on a bad day, on a pissed off, its ok even if you did imperfectly, partially all it matters is you showed up when all your senses against that action. Take way you are keeping on promises you made to yourself no what the excuses. a man succumbs to excuses, is a man who betrays himself which is very pity, shame of himself. outgrow of past mistakes, learn from those mistakes to make and keep promises to yourself of becoming a better version with each passing day. it is like a watching a sun going west, hour hand passing, i.e. it doesn't look like progressing but on sticking on the thing started eventually you realise you came, reached great distances compared to starting point. so keep on doing no matter the excuses, bad days, bad moments, 0 inspiration, 0 show-off so that you trust yourself upon reaching the milestones that you planted. So make promises in order to achieve anything of significant so that it becomes a habit of not breaking the promises. its ok of promise being a small all that matters is, it should be resonating with the things that you wanna get. So no matter the situation, avoid/cease negotiating with your mind of delaying, cancelling actions and waiting for perfect moment, circumstances. stop that Monday, 1st of month, new year eve crap. start now, nature rewards those who start early rather those who wait for perfect moment, constellation. as it goes by saying undoubtedly, luck favors movement rather than perfection. MOVE even when circumstances are not in your favor eventually you became a man who does what is right, even when he doesn't feel it or not interesting in doing it. You don't become that man by overnight nor by fortnight, it takes consistent efforts, anti-negotiable actions to hone yourself of that man. Stop betraying yourself by start making small non-negotiable goal-driven promises. eventually increase the dose/intent of promises as you become foolproof to self-betrayals, thereby you enjoy the version of you who doesn't feel ashamed of oneself. this happens as you keep the promises, builds trust with yourself leading to loving oneself, treating with care and affection. It is a long journey but it is worth taking in order to transform yourself of man that you always dreamed of. Manifest that self of yours by this method of non-negotiable, betrayal-proof promise-keeping to yourself.
