At the some situations, events we accept others invitations, decisions and plans even when its not in our self-interest. We tend to avoid hurting others at the expense of letting ourself hurt more often. This is result of not loving ourselves and caring about others impression. This fault nature of ours gets exploited more often, if we don't define our boundary clearly, instructed our routine clearly they may take our time, energies as granted and exploit in when they got their chance. Practice saying NO when it clearly violates your energies, virtues and time even when it leads to disappointment among them. Let them disappoint often so that they embrace and value your presence next time. seldom they care about your schedules, intentions rather they give attention to their time, reputation and impression. avoid people that drains your energies and not in same harmony as you in terms of mindset, goal set and mainly value-system. Let them disappoint as they deserve it for their misunderstand and not-care of your goals, boundaries. Those who take lightly of your presence, present them your absence as a lesson which itself is the greatest presence one can provide. Become comfortable of making others feel worried, despised of your rejections if it makes your mood lighter and keeps you at peace. Let them learn your protocol and become accustomed to it so that they behave next time and make memorable moments. NO lets them Know your value rather than always nodding YES. The more you showup the less value Your Presence. For getting recognition to your presence/value your presence and to be talk of the town one needs to become comfortable of:

- Saying NO more often(or at appropriate, needy situations)
- drawing boundaries and value self-interest over noise
- let others get disappointed rather than sacrificing our energies, values, routine for others sake(i.e. lame/unnecessary/non-productive purposes)

At the end of the day make sure you aren't disappointing your self, future YOU and your ambitions. let them drive you and yours top-most priority. Don't let them compromise over others approval nor impression. No excuse should cross your mind that lets you exploit your energies. Your energies should, must be drained only for the purpose of your betterment and advancements in your career apart from this everything else is a big NO flag and inefficient recipient of your energies, time and mainly attention.
