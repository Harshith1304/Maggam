Make travelling your hobby and a part of your life to places that you haven't seen before or that are worth seeing multiple times. Cause all we have a one lifetime and its a shame to not explore the world by the time we perish so we need to make sure to travel and collect stories to be told as a bedtime stories to your heirs. Travelling is all about collecting stories that we will come across in that process. So don't give lame excuses when nature has called you to explore it be it in form of some sort of calling through our cousins, friends or that intention/desire that you build gradually. Time is of essence so it is indeed a point to be noted that certain things or places need to be explored at our youthful age cause you might enjoy less when you will old-timer. stop waiting for right time, sufficient money all we need is the zeal to do it and enjoy it to the fullest. Travelling makes us understand who we are, our flaws and our lurky, evil behaviour and point it out as it is in its form. and this activity of travelling introduces us to new situations and makes us meet new people eventually turns to friends, new cultures and lifestyles that we haven't heard before. it makes me pity of those who isn't engaged in this activity as they are missing out on many things which makes them humble and admit nothing comes closer to mother nature. everything which they thought grandeur of something will gets replaced and they laugh at the assumptions that they made previously. they start questioning the beliefs that they hold so far and all this questioning and self-examination starts by travelling to places that he/she hasn't gone before. It pisses me and at the same time makes me pitiful when encountered people who thinks that cinema, celebrities, cricket, politicians >>>>>. it just makes me laugh hard while being pitiful, piss-full at the same time. Get out of your shitty beliefs and take that ride that you been longing to take. money can come and go but time! so it is futile to worry about money and it is THE non-sensical thing to do. there is nothing greater than god, nature. so stop identifying yourselves with something/someone that doesn't give a fuck of what happens to you. dare to stop displaying voyeurism towards celebrity as it doesn't matter of what happens to someone when it isn't in your self-interest. heal your spirits by travelling and soaking in nature.
