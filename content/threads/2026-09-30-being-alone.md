there are some situations where we are comfortable being alone rather than being in group where awkwardness creeps in. It is not always beneficial to be in group or in any herd. Sometimes isolating ourself will enable us to introspect our behaviours. Where are we failing? why we do keep on doing things that we don't want to do? why am i stuck where am I? like these many multiple questions will pop up when we are alone thereby allowing us to improve on drawback areas! So embrace being alone so that it makes us, habituate us being our own best friend, cheer leader and a critic. There are many benefits that spirals around being lonely compared to its demerits which merely arises due to continued isolation even when grouping is necessary. So let there be ceiling to frequency and interval of this activity of isolating oneself in order to be not affected by its disadvantages. the major disadvantages are : less exposure to reality and peer feedback, reaching to a goal in delayed fashion, social anxiety. So in order to avoid these encourage grouping when it is needed, hangout when there is calling for you only in case you didn't feel lonely or it doesn't drag down your energies, values or inefficient passing of time. Where there is a case of being lonely when hangout happens, then avoid hanging with them next time. Ditch places where is no value for your presence, as your mind assumes lack of recognition equivalent to less worth. Make sure to find sweet ratio of isolation to grouping that works best for you. identify activates that needs you being alone and start isolating yourself to those activities. Likewise find activities that needs collaboration and feed there your presence. this way make sure you weren't treated less of your worth and gives time to introspect on your actions and improve on weak areas of your life. Condition yourself to isolation and overtime you feel joyful by enjoying your own company. built a routine of dedicating at least 30 minutes daily of "YOUR" time where are you are alone without any gadgets, distractions, hurry. in this interval rewind your decisions, actions and choices you made that day and look for any improvements that can be made. like this way build a habit and start prioritizing yourself which thereby induces self-worth, self-love and eventually you enjoy that time more than being in a group of friends. In tat stage you are yours best friend, best company. loneliness is out of the box in the events of isolation, it will be alien concept in your life when you are alone but you may feel lonely when you hangout with friends which in that case avoid those that makes you lonely if that interaction doesn't add any value to your life. The point is here always act and take decisions that are in self-interest and adds value to your ambitions which majorly focuses on you being not feeling less worthy that impacts your energies, value-system and virtues.
