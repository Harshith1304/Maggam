Alibi, habit of making excuses for the things we didn't do, or events that we didn't show up for, or promises that we refused to keep. it happens out of fear of what might turn out? what happens if people bully me? Will it lead to defeat? In order to avoid these situations of negative circumstances, we tend to make excuses to ourselves, called alibis. These tendency to defend ourselves for our behaviour of facing uncomfortable situation, it leads to killing our innovation, initiative tenacity. We expect something to happen and do the actions that are inline with our expected outcomes but it is shame when even at the slightest deviation of expected outcome we tend to retreat or stop moving forward by resorting to this habit of creating "ALIBI". Alibis are the destructive mechanism of one's potential where it stunts ones creative forces, persistence. So identify this habit of yours when in the situations you must move forward but your mind tend to make excuses to evade the reality. IT is appreciable to face defeats instead of fearing it and yielding to it. Brute-force sometimes gives you hints and wisdom and chances of acing on next run. Destroy the bridges behind you and move forward. Make sure you either face victory or get valuable lessons from failures instead of making alternative routes and relying upon retreat. Make retreat impossible by emptying your mind with alibis and excuses and make sure to chase the thing that you wanted to chase. Avoid feeding your mind with negative thoughts and negative outcomes that may prevail upon you. Face it as a man-to-man and deal with the outcomes, consequences afterwards. The fear of failure devoid the ability to push his threshold and reach greater heights from a MAN. Find the enemy in your mind that is holding you back from facing risks. Face risks in spite of yielding to alibis. Surround yourself with the environment that makes alibis impossible to pop up in your mind and condition your mind to see the beauty in facing obstacles that certainly triggers your energies to help you push your limits. Have a open-mind, unbiased reaction to situations that occurs to your shortcomings. Address your shortcomings bluntly and you notice it breeds on your alibis. Always keep checking the mind and refrain it from defending itself against taking some actions. Stop defending your weakness and avoid blaming your shortcomings on non-YOU. Take the blame & play fairly the game. Always inspect your mind keenly as it may resort to comfort, gets chickens out. Get hold of your thoughts and transmute them to desirable thoughts that leads to generating effective plans for attaining riches. Exercise your willpower in order to defy your cowardly behavior of your mind in the face of uncertainty and risk of failure. Exercise your willpower and hone it until it builds a wall of immunity in your mind against alibis, excuses, and chickening out in event of taking bold actions. Care to live your life with the rules that you setup rather than it is mind doing the ruling, thereby living in-place of YOU!
