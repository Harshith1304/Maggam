In life, we all come across situations where we choke even when we know what needs to be done, yet do it in unintended way due to anxiety, perfection fetish-ness. It is really draining our energies, mood and time. at the end it really distanced ourself from truly engaging and feeling that task. this is because of high expectations we keep, desiring near-perfect results. which makes even our efforts to not reach our usual high levels. dropping in quality of that thing. so just because of chasing ideal results we end up harming, torturing our soul, mind and our inner energies. so it is ok to let go, ok to not it be good as previous one. let it teach you a new perspective of life lessons. let there be uncomfortableness of not reaching the desired levels. persistence, efforts beyond certain levels/limits even when it is unnecessary(example: writing 15 marks content for 5 marks question) is wasted efforts. So there is no need to be always aiming for bulls-eye just striking that bull alone is far more than enough. over time you may strike that eye on pouring consistent efforts by showing up regularly. so thereby treating your choking behaviour. don't let this behaviour eat your time, energies when it can be consumed for other productive things. Start setting your tone before starting it and set a deadline which should be non negotiable in any case. So that on reptations you tend to get that near-perfect moments, just like streams from hill in monsoon. Dont let those choking moments decide your day, and most importantly YOU. it is done by you, part of you but not the whole of YOU. YOU are not defined by those awkward moments, they are just part of your timeline making not even above 0.00001% of your lifespan. So move on and focus things to be done thereafter. don't stagnate, flow the fuck out. Stagnation attracts bugs, illness(to your mindset, career). Flow uplifts fertile lands and eventually civilization. So don't let any moment, event, decision, circumstances stall you in your pursuit of your predetermined aims. ONTO FORWARD ALWAYS!
