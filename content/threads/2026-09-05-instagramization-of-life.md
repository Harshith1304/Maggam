the instagramization/social mediaization of life, the phenomena that i have been observing since past few years where people not enjoying the present moment instead focusing on capturing and making it public so that people should know that you have this or did this or came here!!

just for sake of having that feel of entitlement, people are getting devoid of that experience of that precious moment. life is all about living in present and fully. No one gives a shit about you that thing you feared what might others think of having it after ages you feel shame, pity on yourself. so grab that thing which completes, which brings you joy and don't fall in trap of the entitlement shit of publishing your deeds in public. there is no such need god is observing you so it is not necessary to prove yourself to others. You should prove to only 2 entitles i.e. YOU and that SUPREME GOD. everyone else should be out of reach. Prove to god that you had made good use of this birth through the vessel that god granted upon us. i don't know whether there is another life to enjoy more in next life or become best in next. but i follow or live by this if we live good enough only one is enough and making sure my life path is aligned with this mindset.

As i strongly believe that god has bestowed a purpose upon each and every one of us, our life mission should be discovering that purpose and gliding in the direction of that purpose. for me i think and strong believe it is to build a legacy that my fore generations should be devoid of pauper, misery and disgrace. I'm a man that believes strongly that one day we gonna leave this material world physically. but we gonna live ages through the legacy we left behind. i will linger on this in much deeper context later.(next day)
