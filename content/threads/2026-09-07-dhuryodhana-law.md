the dhuryodhana law, as we know dhuryodhana is the king leading kurus at time of pandavas-kurus war, who knows what is dharma, righteousness, lawful yet chooses,prefers and walks in the path of adharma. does it ring the bell and are you seeing the pattern we too now-a-days are abiding by this fallacy,law of dhuryodhana. where we know what is right,truthful yet avoids and do that thing which isn't right. say career, indulgences, betrayals, cunningness these are attributes,properties of this law. choosing wrongness over righteousness doesn't it sound much like decisions taken by dhuryodhana too?? dont be fool that caters to his/her own evilish thoughts by acting on it where you know in the heart that it isn't fair!!!

So stop pretending and blaming society for your atrocities where ones action are sole consequences of his own thoughts, virtues and beliefs. we have a free will on what to act and how to behave no one is feeding or controlling your mind to do that action as you are responsible and should be accountable for your own actions,deeds. so it is better and much preferred to take accountability for good deeds rather than to take for unlawful things. so build a value system, virtues , beliefs of your own set which resonates and in same wavelength of the things that do good for society, humanity. identify and develop these traits and act on those traits calling, which thereby imprints greater good on the humankind. which thereby leads to peaceful society where everyone is doing things for greater good for this earth. so dare to instill the dharmic traits which leaves a lasting impression on the actions that you take for betterment of the society. this dhuryodhana law should serve a wakeup call for those who are doing things which aren't good for oneself, society even though someone of them know it is adharma. so wake up and stop going in the direction of flourishing this evilish fallacy, law and heads towards dharmic path for making this world a better place for today, tomorrow.
